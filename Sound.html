  • Put the watch into sync mode, then hold the phone's speaker against it.

  • Each burst carries the exact second it finishes, so the watch can set itself to within a fraction of a second.

Watch settings

Sent as a separate message. Put the watch into sync mode, as for the time, then send. The watch shows OK when it has them. Anything left on “Don't change” stays as it is.

Calibrate level

Do this once the watch is in its case. Lay it case-back down on a flat desk, put it into sync mode, then send without touching it. The watch shows FLAT when done, or MOVED if it was not still enough. It is forgotten if the watch resets.

Audio settings

The tones, bit length and repeats are fixed by the watch firmware. Changing them has to be done in the firmware first, then here to match, or the watch will not decode the signal.

Protocol, for the watch decoder
Modulation
Two-tone FSK, sine, phase continuous. 0 = 2000 Hz, 1 = 2600 Hz, 20 ms per bit.
Frame
80 bits, most significant bit first: 16-bit preamble 1010…10, sync word 0x2DD4, then a 6-byte payload.
Payload
Bytes 0 to 3: Unix time in UTC seconds, big-endian, at the instant the final bit ends. This carries the full date and year as well as the time. In custom mode it is the custom time, counting on from when it was set.
Byte 4: UTC offset of the chosen time zone in 15-minute steps, signed (UK summer time is +4). Local time = Unix time + offset × 900.
Byte 5: CRC-8 over bytes 0 to 4, polynomial 0x07, initial value 0x00.
Repeats
Each repeat finishes on a whole second and carries its own time, with at least 400 ms of silence between them.
Settings frame
Same preamble, length and CRC as the time frame, but sync word 0x4E6B.
Byte 0: face. 0 digital, 1 analog, 2 big digits.
Byte 1: date. 0 24 Sep, 1 Wed 24, 2 24/09, 3 09/24, 4 hidden.
Byte 2: moon phase. 0 northern hemisphere, 1 southern (mirrored), 2 hidden.
Byte 3: action. 0x01 calibrate level (watch lying case-back down), 0x02 clear the calibration, 0xFF none.
Byte 4: wake threshold, 1 to 63 in steps of 31.25 mg (24 = 750 mg is the firmware default). Lower wakes on gentler movement.
Byte 5: CRC-8 over bytes 0 to 4, as above.
0xFF in any field means “leave this setting alone”. Example: 01 FF 01 FF FF FC sets the analog face and southern moon, and leaves the date as it is. FF FF FF 01 FF 25 calibrates level and changes nothing else.
